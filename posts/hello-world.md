Welcome to the **Markdown blog**!

Every file in the `posts/` folder becomes a post. Front matter at the top of the
file sets the title and date:

```markdown
---
title: My Post
date: 2026-08-18
---
```

Things you can do:

- Write posts in your editor and drop them into `posts/`
- Create posts from the [New post](/new) form
- Use any Markdown you like — lists, `code`, [links](https://example.com), and more

> Happy writing.
